UltraAlarm privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

About this policy

UltraAlarm (hereinafter "this app") is an alarm that uses AI to check camera photos to confirm whether you are awake. This policy explains what information this app handles, where it is stored, and where it is sent.

Camera photos

The camera is used only while the alarm is ringing, during back-to-sleep watch, and while the speed test or camera placement screen is open. Photos are checked by the AI on your phone and are not sent outside your phone.

By default, photos used for checking are not kept. They are saved on your phone only when "Save photos" is turned on in settings.

Network cameras

When you register a network camera, the URL, username, and password are saved on your phone. Passwords are encrypted with Android Keystore keys. This app connects directly to the camera without passing through other servers.

Alarm settings and history

Settings such as alarm times and sounds, and history such as wake-up times and whether you fell back asleep, are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to the developer.

External communications

This app communicates only with the following parties. None of these communications send camera photos or alarm history.

  • • Hugging Face (downloading AI used for checks)
  • • Japanese holiday API (holidays-jp.github.io, list of holidays)
  • • Google Play (purchase procedures and checking purchase status)
  • • Network cameras you register

Due to how communication works, these parties receive information such as your phone's IP address. Its handling follows each service's privacy policy.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer never receives payment information. Purchase status is recorded on your phone so that the app can function as the full version even offline.

Advertising and analytics

This app does not display ads. It also does not include tools for usage analytics or crash reporting.

Device backups

If Android backup is turned on, this app's settings and history may be included in your Google Account backup. This backup is managed by Google and cannot be viewed by the developer. AI models and network camera passwords are not included. Please re-enter your password on a new phone.

Data deletion

If you uninstall this app or clear this app's storage in Android settings, all information stored on your phone will be deleted.

Cloud checking

In the future, we plan to launch a Cloud AI plan that sends photos to a server for checking. When launched, the destination and photo handling will be added to this policy, and we will ask for your confirmation in the app before you start using it. Unless you choose cloud checking, photos will never leave your phone.

Changes to this policy

When changing this policy, we will update the in-app display and published page, noting the date of the change.

Contact us

For inquiries regarding this policy, please send an email to the address listed in our Google Play store listing.
