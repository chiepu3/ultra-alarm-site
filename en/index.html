UltraAlarm for Android

An alarm that checks
whether you’re awake.

Camera photos and AI help check whether you’re up. The alarm stops when you’re awake and rings again if you fall back asleep during the watch.

View on Google Play How to use

Keeps watch after
the alarm stops.

AI checks whether you’re awake to stop the alarm, then watches for you falling back asleep. You can also stop the alarm manually.

  • Camera photos and AI checks

    The alarm rings at the time you set. AI checks photos from the camera and stops the alarm when it determines that you’re awake.

  • Back-to-sleep watch

    When enabled, checks continue after the alarm stops. Set the watch time for each alarm: 60 min by default, up to 120 min.

  • Choose your camera

    Use your phone’s front or rear camera, an RTSP network camera, or a still-image URL.

  • Checks run on your phone

    Camera photos are not sent outside your phone. Photos used for checks are not saved by default. See the privacy policy for details.

Getting started

Use the free trial to check how the app runs on your phone and how long checks take before purchasing. AI checks are available through a one-time purchase.

  1. Prepare permissions and AI

    Follow the app’s instructions to allow notifications, camera access, and other required permissions. Download the AI used for checks.

  2. Place the camera and test

    Position the camera far enough away to show the room and your whole body in bed. Run a speed test to check the result and time taken.

  3. Set your alarm and watch time

    Choose the alarm time, repeat schedule, and sound. If you want to use back-to-sleep watch, set how long it should continue.

Phone requirements

Check speed and availability depend on your phone’s performance. The Android version alone does not determine whether it will work.

Android
Requires Android 10 or later. Development and testing target the Galaxy S and Z series.
Storage and connection
The initial AI download is several GB. You need enough free storage and an internet connection for the download.
Camera placement
Make sure the camera can see the room and your whole body in bed. Complete darkness reduces check accuracy, so use a night light or other dim lighting.
Battery settings
Battery optimization and manufacturer power-saving settings may prevent alarms from ringing. Follow the app’s instructions to check these settings.

On some phones, AI checks may be slow or may not work. Please also read the terms of use.
